king hemesh

/kɪŋ ˈhɛmɪʃ/

noun

1. figurative

The undisputed ruler of all dynasties, seven kingdoms, and everything in between.

A legendary king. An unbeatable warrior. A menace to his enemies and a problem for his enemies’ enemies.

“One does not simply challenge King Hemesh.”

2. mythology

Born to rule. Destined to conquer.

origin

Ancient. Possibly mythical.
